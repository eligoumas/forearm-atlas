What you're seeing. A camera can't see under skin. The app tracks 21 points on your hand (and your elbow when it can find it), fits a standard anatomical model to them, and estimates which muscles are working from how fast and how far each joint bends. Shapes and activity are approximations for learning, not a medical image. Video is processed in your browser and is not uploaded. Keys: 1 hand, 2 wrist, 3 forearm, V change view. Drag the 3D model to turn it; it copies your finger movements.
