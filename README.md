# Forearm Atlas

**Open it: https://eligoumas.github.io/forearm-atlas/**

Hold your forearm up to the camera and move your fingers. A 3D model of the muscles, tendons, bones, nerves and vessels
follows your arm, and the muscles doing the work light up. Without a camera, a demo hand shows how it works.
Video stays in your browser and is never uploaded.

Works best in Chrome or Edge on a laptop or desktop. The 3D model is built in Blender by `blender/build_forearm.py`.
Shapes and activity are approximations for learning, not a medical image.
